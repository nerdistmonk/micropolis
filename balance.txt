MICROPOLIS BALANCE NOTES (devworks)
====================================

Status

  and tiered landfill upkeep (see Changes in effect).
- Policing overhaul is committed on devworks as e2fe343 (see Policing).

- The "LA" city is a procedural map (GenerateSomeCity(12345), then ClearMap()), not real Los Angeles.

Changes in effect (scratch only)
--------------------------------
1. Tax rates (core/s_sim.c:630, 637)
   - FLevels {3.0, 2.5, 2.0}   (stock {1.4, 1.2, 0.8})
   - Tax divisor /60            (stock /120)
2. Service costs: flat x100 per building (stock). Population scaling was tried and removed.
3. Roads: stock upkeep (CalcRoadFund, core/s_sim.c:618-622). Not scaled.
4. Landfill storage: LANDFILL_STAGE_CAP 800 (stock 400), so a block holds 2,400 (stock 1,200).
5. Landfill cost is tiered by block fill (scratch: s_landfill_tier.c LandfillTieredCost,
   used in s_sim_tier.c CollectTax):
   - empty block: 0
   - 1/3 to 2/3 full range: 33 / 66 per block
   - full: 100 per block
6. Health: stock 80/20 residential penalty (core/s_health.c:14-15).
   A 95/5 test changed tax by 0 to 7 a year, so it is not a useful lever.

Policing (committed e2fe343, core/s_police.c)
---------------------------------------------
Old model: PoliceMap was a smoothed radius around each station, and CrimeScan subtracted it
from crime everywhere. Coverage was not limited, so one station covered a whole district.

New model:
1. Station capacity (zones served):
   - 24 with power and water, 12 without one (degraded), at full funding.
   - Scaled linearly by PoliceEffect (funding ratio, core/s_sim.c:688-689).
     Unfunded = 0. Half funding = half capacity (rounded down).
   - The road-access penalty (FindPRoad) was removed.
2. Assignment: each residential, commercial, and industrial zone goes to the nearest
   station (squared distance) with spare capacity. Zones are processed in map order.
3. Crime: covered zones get 0. Uncovered cells get no police term.
   Crime base is 128 - LandValue + PopDensity (unchanged).
4. Overlay: green = covered, red = uncovered (sdl3/sdl3_video.c).
5. Inspector: station shows "Demand" (zones assigned) and "Capacity".

Verified with scratch probe (probe_police.c) on saves at 32 cycles:
- Dingberg-1.cot, 3 stations, 24 zones, all covered at full funding.
- Dingberg.cot, 1 station (10,2), capacity 24, 22 zones, all covered.
- Funding sweep on Dingberg.cot, 1 station (10,2), 22 zones:
    PoliceEffect 0    -> capacity 0,  covered 0/22,  uncovered mean crime 117.4
    PoliceEffect 500  -> capacity 12, covered 12/22
    PoliceEffect 1000 -> capacity 24, covered 22/22

Fire (committed 60ff8f9, core/s_fire.c)
---------------------------------------
Old model: each station added to a smoothed FireStMap (3 smoothing passes over 8x8 blocks).
FireRate was the result, and it set burn-out speed (Rate 3/2/1 by strength, 10 without a station).
Stations also lost strength without a road (FindPRoad).

New model:
1. Station capacity (zones served): same as police.
   - 24 with power and water, 12 without one, scaled linearly by FireEffect.
     Unfunded = 0. The road penalty (FindPRoad) was removed.
2. Assignment: each residential, commercial, and industrial zone goes to the nearest
   station with spare capacity, citywide, no radius limit.
3. Burn-out: covered tiles with water burn out at Rate 2. All other tiles use the
   stock Rate 10. The old strength tiers (3, 2, 1) are gone.
4. Growth: the citywide valve penalty for underfunded fire (s_eval.c:277) is kept.
5. Overlay: green = covered, red = uncovered (sdl3/sdl3_video.c), matching police.
6. Inspector: station shows "Fire Demand" (zones assigned) and "Capacity".
   FireStMap and FireRate are removed.

Verified with scratch probe (probe_fire.c):
- Dingberg-1.cot, 2 stations, 24 zones, all covered at full funding.
- Dingberg-1.cot at FireEffect 500: 12 per station, 24/24 covered.
- Dingberg-1.cot at FireEffect 0: 0 covered, 24 uncovered, no fast-burn tiles.

Landfill (core/s_landfill.c, uncommitted)
-----------------------------------------
Old model: each CityTime tick added TotalPop / 8 to a shared pool (TrashPending, capped at
100000). Each road-connected block took 40 x landfillPercent per tick. A full block triggered
auto-placement of a new block beside it.

New model (capacity-based):
1. Capacity: each road-connected landfill block services 9 developed zones, scaled by
   landfillPercent (budget slider), as police and fire stations are. Half budget = 4.5 zones
   per block. Unconnected blocks add nothing.
2. Demand: residential, commercial, and industrial zone centres citywide (same predicate as
   police). Demand = zone count.
3. Each tick, compare demand with total capacity:
   - Overloaded (demand > capacity): each connected block that is not full gains 40 waste.
     Demand beyond capacity is uncollected.
   - Under capacity (demand < capacity): each block holding waste loses 40 on every third
     tick (CityTime % 3 == 0). Net empty rate is one third of the fill rate.
   - Balanced (demand == capacity): nothing changes.
4. Storage: 2,400 per block (4 stages, unchanged). TrashPending is the total waste across all
   blocks, recomputed each tick. The land-value penalty, garbage problem, and pile/capacity
   headlines read it unchanged.
5. No capacity (no connected block) with demand present: trash piles up unserviced at 40 per
   tick, capped at 5,000 (UnservicedTrash). It drains at 40 per tick once demand is gone.
   It counts toward TrashPending, so the penalties apply. The "landfill full" headline
   (LandfillAtCapacity) fires in this state too.
6. Removed: TotalPop-based generation, the 100,000 pool cap, and auto-placement. Blocks are
   placed by the player only.
7. Unchanged: tiered upkeep, stage pollution. UnservicedTrash is saved in MiscHis[77..78]
   beside TrashPending.
8. Landfill blocks take no power or water. They are excluded from power demand, water demand,
   and the power/water overlay flash icons (TILE_IS_LANDFILL). The inspector shows citywide
   capacity vs demand (LandfillSystemStats) for a clicked landfill.

Verified with scratch probe (probe_landfill.c), one connected block, Legacy/freds.cty:
- Under, zones 0, capacity 9: 1200 -> 800 in 30 ticks (10 steps of 40).
- Overloaded, zones 5, percent 0: +40 per tick, stage tiles change at 800/1600/2400,
  caps at 2400, LandfillAtCapacity set.
- Balanced, zones 9, capacity 9: holds at 800.
- Half budget, capacity 4.5: 5 zones overload (+40/tick); 4 zones drain.

Open (thresholds not retuned, needs a decision):
- Land-value penalty (/250, max 20) saturates at 5,000 stored waste (about two full blocks).
- Pile headline TRASH_PILE_LEVEL 5,000, re-arm 1,000 (core/s_msg.c:24-25).
- Garbage problem (/20) saturates at 5,100 stored waste.
- Unserviced piles (no connected landfill) reach the 5,000 cap after 125 ticks at 40/tick.
  Verified with probe_unserviced.c: 5 zones, no landfill -> 5,000 after 200 ticks; zero
  demand drains to 0.

Failure mode (all variants)
---------------------------
- The city ends with 4 landfill blocks. Garbage starts accumulating around year 18-20.
- Land value falls (about 64 to 37), which cuts tax income by roughly half.
- Expansion needs a clear 3x3 patch beside a full block. The packed map has none after year 18.
  This is not yet confirmed in code.
- Fix options not yet tested: reserve more landfill space at the start, or raise capacity further.

Known gaps
----------
- Only four seeds, 25 years, GameLevel 0 (easy).
- Map is procedural, so zone counts are fixed at about 58 to 91 cells.
- The reload gap (5,560 vs 5,700) is unexplained.
- Fire assignment has the same order-dependence as police (map order, not global nearest-first).
- Fire coverage uses the same zone footprints as police. Burning roads beside a covered zone
  count as covered.
- Fire burn-out rate (Rate 2 covered, 10 stock) is a new number and has not been tuned in a long run.
- Police assignment is order-dependent: earlier zones in map order take capacity first.
  A global nearest-first pairing would be fairer. Not implemented.
- "Demand" in the inspector is zones assigned, not unmet demand.
- Unpowered zones still count toward station capacity.
- Covered crime is 0. Crime is tracked per half-resolution cell, so a cell touching
  any covered tile counts as covered. This slightly over-covers edges.
- Policing crime tuning is not checked against long runs (no 25-year policing test yet).
- Balance changes are not proposed for the core. Any change needs review first.
